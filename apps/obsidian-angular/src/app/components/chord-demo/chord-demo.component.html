🎸 Magic 18 Chord Chart Demo

Interactive guitar chord learning system - Starting with chord I (C major)

🎯 How to Use:

  1. Select a Roman Numeral: Click on any Roman numeral (I, ii, iii, IV, V, vi, vii°) to see the chord
  2. View the Fretboard: See the chord positions highlighted on the guitar fretboard
  3. Change Keys: Use the key selector to transpose the entire chart
  4. Play Chords: Click the play button to hear the chord (audio integration coming)
  5. Learn Colors: Green=Root, Red=Third, Blue=Fifth, Grey=Seventh
